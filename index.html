About me

I'm a first year Ph.D. student at UCSD working with Ranjit Jhala. In my current project I'm extending Flux with the capability to emit verification conditions in Lean so that users can interactively prove them when automatic verification fails. I'm broadly interested in type systems and formal verification.

Before coming to UCSD I was a software engineer in San Francisco working for Asana Inc, and before that I did my undergrad at Williams College, majoring in Computer Science and Mathematics.
