About me

I'm a second year Ph.D. student at UCSD working with Ranjit Jhala. I'm broadly interested in type systems and formal verification. Some stuff I'm working on:

  • Tinkering with a liquid-type system in Lean as a means of proof automation
  • Continuing to work on Flex together with the Flux extension for emitting constraints in Lean to enable interactive proving where automation fails
  • Still hacking on PastaLean from my summer internship: a system to translate Python into Lean to enable verification

Before coming to UCSD, I was a software engineer in San Francisco working for Asana Inc, and before that I did my undergrad at Williams College, majoring in Computer Science and Mathematics.
